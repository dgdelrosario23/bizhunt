ABOUT BIZHUNT

Local discoveries. Stronger communities.

BizHunt is built around a simple idea: discovering and supporting local businesses should be rewarding. We are bringing local discovery, digital loyalty and community connections into one experience.

For curious locals

Make it easier to discover businesses, revisit favorites and organize participating rewards. Our goal is to help people get more out of the places they already visit and the ones they have yet to discover.

DiscoverCollect rewardsConnect

For business owners

Small businesses deserve accessible tools to showcase what they do and build lasting customer relationships. BizHunt provides merchant features for creating campaigns and reviewing activity.

Showcase your businessBuild loyaltyUnderstand engagement

For communities

BizHunt supports community connections through features such as community groups and partner perks where available. We believe local commerce grows stronger when people discover and support one another.

Community groupsPartner opportunitiesLocal connections

Where we’re starting

BizHunt is a developing platform with an initial focus on Angeles City and Pampanga. Availability and features may evolve as more businesses and communities participate. We welcome feedback from people exploring the app and business owners interested in joining.
