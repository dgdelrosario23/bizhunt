Frequently asked questions

Questions about BizHunt?

A few things to know before joining the hunt.

What is BizHunt?

BizHunt is a local business discovery and loyalty platform. Customers can explore businesses, follow places, and collect eligible loyalty stamps and rewards from participating merchants.

How do businesses join BizHunt?

Business owners can create an account, set up a business profile, and use the merchant tools available to their account. Some features or verification steps may be required before a listing is fully active.

How do digital loyalty stamps work?

Participating businesses create loyalty campaigns with their own rules. Customers earn stamps for eligible transactions after merchant approval, then redeem rewards when the campaign requirements are met.

Do customers need a BizHunt account?

Customers can explore public information where available. An account is required for personalized activities such as collecting stamps, saving businesses, and joining communities.

Can businesses offer promotions and vouchers?

BizHunt supports merchant promotions and vouchers, including eligible community partner offers. Availability depends on the merchant's setup and campaign rules.

Is BizHunt free for businesses?

Merchant pricing and plan details have not been announced yet. Please check the official BizHunt channels for confirmed pricing before making a decision.
