For explorers

What is BizHunt?

BizHunt helps people discover local businesses, earn eligible loyalty rewards, find offers and connect with communities.

Do I need an account?

An account is required for activities such as collecting stamps, saving businesses, joining communities and managing a business.

How do I collect stamps?

Check the business’s active campaign, make an eligible purchase and show My QR for the merchant to approve your stamp.

How do I redeem my reward?

Open the completed card in Wallet and follow the redemption instructions. The participating merchant verifies the reward according to its campaign rules.

Can I earn rewards at every listed business?

No. Rewards are available at businesses with active participating campaigns. Check the individual listing and terms before visiting.

Can I use paper loyalty cards in BizHunt?

Existing paper cards are governed by the merchant’s own rules. Ask the merchant whether it offers a BizHunt digital program or a way to handle existing stamps.

For businesses

How does my business join?

Create an account, set up your business profile and follow the available onboarding steps in BizHunt. Listing activation may require further information.

Who sets the reward and stamp rules?

Each merchant sets campaign requirements, the stamp goal and reward. Customers should review those terms before participating.

Can I publish promotions?

Participating merchants can use the available merchant tools to publish offers and vouchers, subject to their account’s available features.

Is BizHunt free for businesses?

Pricing and plan details have not been announced on the official website. Check official BizHunt channels for confirmed information.

What is BizHunt VIP?

BizHunt VIP is a premium guest engagement concept for businesses interested in exclusive privileges and thoughtful loyalty. Visit the VIP page to learn more and submit an inquiry.
